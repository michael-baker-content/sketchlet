# make yourself at home

**I'm Michael Baker, the creator of sketchlet.**

Recently I had an idea for a daily drawing game in the Wordle style ... and now it's a website! Thank you to large language models and programming classes.

This app is playable now, but note that it is in active development. Feel free to provide feedback if you have ideas about how to make the site more fun.

**New feature — keep your drawings together across devices**

You can now sign in to sketchlet with your email or Google account.

Keep your drawings together across devices. Sign in on your phone, tablet, or computer to access the same profile and drawing collection. Your profile photo follows you too, but stays private—only you can see it.

Bring your earlier drawings along. Your profile lets you review and link guest drawings from this browser. If you drew on another browser, device, or the old site address, you can create a transfer code there and bring those drawings into your account. If two drawings share a date, you choose which one to keep.

Guest drawing is still welcome. You don’t need an account to draw or browse. Unfinished canvases still save on the device where you started them.

Accounts also lay the groundwork for friend groups, which are coming next: an easier way to find each other’s drawings.

Open profile to sign in and get settled.
